How it works

From raw video to a checked record

No manual logging. Add a debate, and DebateLens does the slow part so your team can focus on judgement.

Add the debate

Upload a recording or paste a YouTube link. Long programmes are fine: hustings, TV debates, panels, podcasts.

We transcribe & extract

A timestamped transcript separated by speaker, with every checkable factual claim pulled out and prioritised.

Review the evidence

Each claim gets a preliminary verdict with linked sources. You confirm, correct, and decide what gets published.

▶

Transcript synced to video

Click any line to jump to that moment. Search the whole debate in seconds.

◉

Speaker identification

Voices are separated automatically and named from the debate itself or by you, never guessed from appearance.

✓

Claims & evidence

Supported, contradicted, mixed or unverifiable, always with the sources behind it.

▬

Claim timeline

See where the contested moments cluster across the whole programme at a glance.

❝

Quote checking

Pin a quotation a speaker attributes to someone else and mark it verified, altered or misattributed.

★

Viral moments

Mark the clips people will share and present them with the context that came next.

Story mode
