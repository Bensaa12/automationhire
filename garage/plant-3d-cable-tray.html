FAQ

Why do parts come in circular by default in Plant 3D?

AutoCAD Plant 3D ships the rectangular "squareconduits" shape scripts inside variants.zip, but does not register them in ScriptGroup.xml. Without registration the router falls back to the circular pipe engine. This pack adds 32 CSQ entries to ScriptGroup.xml under matching pipe/elbow/tee groups so the router recognises them.

Which Plant 3D versions are supported?

AutoCAD Plant 3D 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027. The shape scripts are present in variants.zip on all of these versions. If your install is on a non-default drive, pass -Plant3DContent "D:\..." to install.ps1.

Do I keep working if I later update Plant 3D?

Yes. Only ScriptGroup.xml is edited on the Plant 3D install (with a timestamped backup). If a Plant 3D update rewrites that file, just re-run install.ps1 to re-apply the patch.

Can I modify the catalog?

Absolutely. Open the .pcat in Plant 3D's Catalog Builder and add sizes, change material metadata, adjust prices — whatever you need. The licence permits internal modification.
