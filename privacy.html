This Privacy Policy explains how AutomationHire.co.uk ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data. We are committed to complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Please read this policy carefully before using our website or services.

1. Who We Are

AutomationHire.co.uk is an online directory and marketplace connecting businesses with AI automation agencies and freelancers. We also run Jarvis Academy, an AI learning service for students and families, which has its own rules for children’s data (see section 10). We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

Our Details

  • Website: automationhire.co.uk
  • Contact email: privacy@automationhire.co.uk
  • Registered in: England and Wales

If you have any questions about how we handle your personal data, please contact us at the email address above before raising a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

2. Data We Collect

We collect different data depending on how you use our website. We only collect what is necessary for the purpose stated.

2.1 — Visitors (Browsing the Directory)

  • IP address and approximate location (via server logs)
  • Browser type, device type, operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, referral source
  • Search queries entered on the directory

This data is collected automatically and is used only for analytics and security purposes. It is not used to identify you personally.

2.2 — Clients (Requesting Quotes or Getting Matched)

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Company name (optional)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Project description, budget and timeline
  • Tools and platforms you currently use

2.3 — Providers (Listing Your Business)

  • Full name and business name
  • Email address and password (encrypted)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Business description, services, tools and industries
  • Website URL and LinkedIn profile
  • Location (city and country)
  • Pricing information
  • Portfolio content and case studies
  • Certifications uploaded
  • Billing information (processed by Stripe — we do not store card details)

2.4 — Newsletter Subscribers

  • Email address
  • Date of subscription and source page

2.5 — Reviews

  • Reviewer name, company and role
  • Email address (not published publicly)
  • Review text and star rating

2.6 — Jarvis Academy (Students and Parents)

  • Account: email address, first name or nickname, education level (primary, secondary or university), account type (student or parent) and whether the age/guardian declaration was ticked
  • Learning records: for each topic practised, the subject, topic, a confidence score, the number of attempts and correct answers, and a short note about a common mistake
  • Session records: a random session ID, the education level, the number of questions asked, and start and last-activity times
  • Family links: which parent account is linked to which student account, and any unused one-time link code
  • Try Jarvis (no account): the questions you type are sent to our AI provider to produce a reply. We do not store them. Your IP address is held in server memory for up to an hour to limit abuse

We do not store the text of Jarvis Academy conversations. Details are in section 10.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We only share your data with trusted third-party service providers that help us operate AutomationHire. All processors are bound by data processing agreements and comply with UK GDPR.

5.1 — Supabase (Database & Authentication)

We use Supabase to store all user accounts, Jarvis Academy learning records and directory data, and to send account confirmation emails. Supabase is hosted on AWS infrastructure in the EU (Ireland). Supabase Privacy Policy →

5.2 — Stripe (Payment Processing)

All subscription payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. We never receive or store your card details — they go directly to Stripe. Stripe is PCI DSS Level 1 compliant. Stripe Privacy Policy →

5.3 — Resend (Transactional Email)

We use Resend to deliver transactional emails such as lead notifications, quote confirmations and welcome emails. Resend processes your email address and the email content on our behalf. Resend Privacy Policy →

5.4 — Vercel (Website Hosting)

Our website is hosted on Vercel Inc. servers. Vercel processes server logs including IP addresses as part of normal web hosting operations. Vercel Privacy Policy →

5.5 — Anthropic (AI Tutor)

Jarvis Academy replies are generated by AI models provided by Anthropic, PBC. When you ask Jarvis something, your message (and recent messages in the same conversation, plus your first name and a short summary of topics you find hard if you are signed in) is sent to Anthropic to produce the reply. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models, and it may be kept for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. See Anthropic Privacy Policy →. Please do not type personal details such as your full name, address or school into Jarvis.

5.6 — Legal Disclosure

7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of them, contact us at privacy@automationhire.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

Right of Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you (commonly called a Subject Access Request or SAR). We will provide this free of charge in a commonly used electronic format.

Right to Rectification

If any personal data we hold about you is inaccurate or incomplete, you have the right to have it corrected. Providers can update most data directly from their dashboard.

Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data where there is no compelling reason for us to continue processing it. Note that we may need to retain some data for legal or contractual reasons (e.g. payment records for 7 years).

Right to Restrict Processing

You can ask us to restrict how we use your data while a dispute is being resolved — for example if you contest the accuracy of your data or have objected to processing.

Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format (e.g. JSON or CSV) and to transmit it to another service.

Right to Object

Where we rely on legitimate interests as our legal basis, you have the right to object to processing. We will stop unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on your consent (e.g. newsletter), you can withdraw consent at any time by clicking "Unsubscribe" in any email or contacting us directly. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

  • Website: ico.org.uk
  • Phone: 0303 123 1113
  • Post: ICO, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF

We do ask that you contact us first so we have the opportunity to resolve your concern directly.

10. Children's Privacy and Jarvis Academy

The directory, quotes and provider features of AutomationHire are a business-to-business service for adults aged 18 and over. Jarvis Academy is different: it is a learning service designed for students, including children. This section applies to Jarvis Academy and takes priority over the rest of this policy for it.

10.1 — Who can use Jarvis Academy

  • Aged 13 and over: may create their own student account.
  • Under 13: may use Jarvis only through an account that a parent or guardian sets up and supervises, who consents on the child’s behalf.
  • We ask each new user to confirm this with a tick box. We do not independently verify age or identity. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account without a parent or guardian’s involvement, we will delete it.
  • Parent or guardian accounts are for adults aged 18 and over.

10.2 — What we keep, and what we don’t

  • We keep the account, learning, session and family-link data listed in section 2.6.
  • We do not store the text of conversations with Jarvis. The messages are sent to our AI provider to generate a reply (section 5.5) and are not saved in our database.
  • We do not sell children’s data, show advertising in Jarvis Academy, or use it to build marketing profiles.

10.3 — Scores and automated assessment

After you answer one of Jarvis’s questions, an AI model estimates whether your answer was right and updates a confidence score for that topic. This is used only to personalise tutoring and to build the progress summary. It has no legal or similarly significant effect on anyone, is not used for grading, selection or discipline, and can be wrong. You can ask us for a human review, to correct it, or to object to it by emailing privacy@automationhire.co.uk.

10.4 — Parent and guardian view

  • A student can create a one-time code (valid for 7 days) and give it to a parent or guardian, who enters it on their own parent account. A parent can link up to 4 children.
  • A linked parent sees a summary only: the child’s first name and level, how many sessions and questions this week, the last active date, and subject and topic scores. They never see the child’s conversations.
  • The student’s own screen shows how many parents can see their summary. Either side can stop sharing at any time.

10.5 — Safety and supervision

  • Jarvis is instructed to keep content age-appropriate, to stay on learning topics, and to encourage a student who seems upset or at risk to talk to a trusted adult.
  • Because we do not store or monitor conversations, we cannot see or step in if a child raises a safety concern with Jarvis. We recommend that children under 13 use Jarvis with an adult nearby.
  • AI can make mistakes. Check important facts with a teacher or textbook.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 999. Children can talk to Childline on 0800 1111, and adults worried about a child can call the NSPCC helpline on 0808 800 5000.

10.6 — Access, correction and deletion

A student, or a parent or guardian on a child’s behalf, can ask us to give a copy of, correct or delete Jarvis Academy data by emailing privacy@automationhire.co.uk from the account email address. We will reply within one month. Deleting an account removes its learning records, sessions and family links. Rights under UK GDPR are described in section 7.

11. Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures including:

  • All data in transit encrypted via TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS)
  • All data at rest encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes — we cannot see your password
  • Row-level security (RLS) policies ensure providers can only access their own data. Jarvis Academy tables allow no direct public access and are reached only through our server
  • Payment card data handled exclusively by Stripe — we never touch it
  • API keys and secrets stored as environment variables, never in code
  • Access to the production database limited to essential personnel only

Despite these measures, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to security@automationhire.co.uk.

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and notify affected individuals without undue delay as required under UK GDPR Article 34.
