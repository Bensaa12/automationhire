  2. Run HireMotion-Setup-1.0.0.exe.
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. This appears for new apps from small publishers that haven't bought a code-signing certificate yet.
  4. Accept the licence and click Install. HireMotion opens from your desktop or Start menu.
  5. Paste your licence key when HireMotion first opens and click Activate. Just once per PC.

FAQ

Is it really just £1?

Yes — one payment, no subscription, no account, no adverts and no watermark on your carousels.

How do I post the carousel on Instagram?

HireMotion saves each slide as its own file, numbered 01, 02, 03 and so on, in a new folder. Send them to your phone, start a new Instagram post, choose "Select multiple" and pick them in order. Use the PNGs for a still carousel, or the MP4s for slides that move.

Can I use my own photos and colours?

Yes. Every slide has its own words and photo spots, and the Brand tab sets your business name, Instagram handle, website and five colours that restyle the whole template.

What's in Pro?

Story and Reel videos (9:16), four motion styles, one video of the whole carousel for Reels, TikTok and Shorts with transitions between slides, sound effects timed to the animation, your own background music, animated stickers and panorama carousels that swipe as one scene. £9.99, or £8.99 if you've bought HireMotion.
