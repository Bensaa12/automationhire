FAQ

I already bought HireCast for £1. Do I pay £9.99?

No — paste your HireCast licence key into the upgrade box at the top of this page and Pro costs £8.99, the difference. You get a new Pro key; your videos and projects carry straight on.

Does Pro go free at 10,000 subscribers too?

No. Standard HireCast becomes free for everyone at 10,000 subscribers. Pro, with the webcam features, stays a paid one-off — it's what funds the next apps.

Can I move or hide the camera after recording?

Yes. The webcam is recorded as its own file alongside the screen, so in the editor you can change the bubble's shape, corner, size, mirror and border at any time — or hide it on some clips (split first to hide it for just part of a recording). The preview shows exactly what the export will look like.

Does it watermark my videos?

No. There's no watermark and no export limit, in Standard or Pro.

Can I record system audio and my mic at the same time?

Yes. HireCast mixes your computer's sound and your microphone into one track, with separate volume controls and live level meters. If your chosen mic isn't sending any sound (common with Elgato Wave Link or NVIDIA Broadcast setups), it warns you before you record and suggests the right input.

How does auto-zoom work?

When you record a whole screen, HireCast also records where your mouse is and when you click. One button then adds smooth zooms around your clicks that follow the pointer. You can drag the zoom bars on the timeline to stretch or move them, or add your own.
