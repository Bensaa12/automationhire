FAQ

I already bought HireMotion for £1. Do I pay £9.99?

No — paste your HireMotion licence key into the upgrade box at the top of this page and Pro costs £8.99, the difference. You get a new Pro key; paste it into HireMotion and the Pro features unlock straight away.

Can I use any music?

Use music you have the rights to. Instagram and TikTok can mute or block videos with copyrighted songs. Free libraries such as the YouTube Audio Library and Pixabay Music work well. The built-in sound effects are AutomationHire's own and free to use in anything you make.

Where do I get more Lottie animations?

HireMotion includes four (a tick, a pulse, a swipe arrow and sparkles) in your brand colour. You can also import any Lottie .json file — check the licence of animations you download, for example on LottieFiles.

How long does a Reel take to export?

About a minute for a six-slide carousel on a recent PC, longer on older ones. Everything is rendered on your PC; nothing is uploaded.
