FAQ

I already bought HirePDF for £1. Do I pay £9.99?

No — paste your HirePDF licence key into the upgrade box at the top of this page and Pro costs £8.99, the difference. You get a new Pro key; paste it into HirePDF and the Pro features unlock straight away.

How much smaller will my PDF get?

It depends what's inside. Scans and PDFs full of photos usually shrink a lot — often to a tenth of the size. PDFs that are mostly text are already small, so they won't change much. Light keeps photos looking good for email; Strong makes the smallest file.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Unlike online PDF websites, HirePDF runs entirely on your PC. Your contracts, bank statements and IDs never leave it, there's no file-size limit, and there's no account to create.

Does it change my original PDF?

No. HirePDF always saves a new file, for example Report (merged).pdf or Contract - pages 1-3.pdf, and never overwrites an existing file.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. If a PDF is locked, HirePDF tells you so — remove the password in the program that made it, then add it again.
