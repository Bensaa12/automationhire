Install in a minute

  1. Buy above — you'll come straight back here with a Download button and your licence key.
  2. Run HireConvert-Setup-1.0.0.exe.
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. This appears for new apps from small publishers that haven't bought a code-signing certificate yet.
  4. Accept the licence and click Install. HireConvert opens from your desktop or Start menu, and you'll find Convert with HireConvert when you right-click a video, song or photo.
  5. Paste your licence key when you first open HireConvert: click the badge at the top right, paste, Activate. Just once per PC.

FAQ

Is it really just £1?

Yes — one payment, no subscription, no account, no adverts, no watermarks. You also get the Pro features free for your first 14 days.

What happens after the 14-day Pro trial?

HireConvert keeps working with everything in the £1 edition: video, audio and photo conversions, video to MP3 and iPhone photos. Shrinking videos to a size, GIFs and resizing need Pro, which is £8.99 for £1 owners.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Unlike online converters, HireConvert runs entirely on your PC. Your videos and photos never leave it, there's no file-size limit, and there's no account to create.

Does it change my original files?

No. HireConvert always saves a new file, for example Holiday.mp4 next to Holiday.MOV. If a file with that name already exists, it adds a number instead of overwriting it.

Can I convert lots of files at once?

Yes. Drop in as many files as you like, or a whole folder, and pick "Convert all to". HireConvert works through them one by one and shows each one's progress.
